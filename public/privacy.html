ConvoAutopsy Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Local data and backups

The web app uses a browser-local guest profile. There is no ConvoAutopsy account or backend account for the web app. Saved reports, source text you choose to retain, response drafts, preferences, and consent choices are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly share them. A versioned recovery envelope keeps all valid legacy report buckets separate; only the previously selected legacy profile can migrate into current history. The dashboard shows recovery counts without other-profile content until you explicitly export the private recovery file. Export leaves the browser copy intact. Depending on your browser, iOS, and iCloud settings, local app data may be included in browser, device, or iCloud backups.

On-device and optional AI features

On-device analysis and drafts need no network transmission. After consent and exact-data review, an optional AI analysis sends to the ConvoAutopsy Cloudflare service: schema version, consent version, an installation token, and each reviewed message sender and message text. The service forwards only the reviewed message sender and message text to Groq. An optional AI response-drafting request sends the service schema version, consent version, the installation token, the chosen response sender, goal, and tone, the analysis mode, intensity score, and conflict mode, and—for each message—the message sender, edited message text, pattern, ego state, and edited possible interpretation. The service forwards the content and drafting fields to Groq; it does not forward schema version, consent version, the installation token, or analysis mode to Groq. Technical identifier values are not displayed in the review. The optional output can be incomplete, biased, or wrong and is not a factual conclusion about people or relationships.

Purchases

In the native app, Free verification can send a pseudonymous RevenueCat app-user ID even without a subscription. Purchase and entitlement checks can send that ID and purchase and entitlement history to RevenueCat. The ConvoAutopsy service may cache verified entitlement status for up to five minutes. RevenueCat and Apple apply their own privacy policies to information they process.

Safety, quotas, and operations

The service HMAC-derives rate and quota identifiers from pseudonymous customer, installation, network, and route signals instead of storing those raw identifiers in its rate and quota keys. It retains bounded quota usage rows, a daily provider budget, two-minute recovery leases, short-lived provider-failure and circuit state, and content-free operational metrics. These records enforce fair-use, reliability, abuse-prevention, and cost limits and do not contain conversation text.

What ConvoAutopsy does not do

ConvoAutopsy does not sell personal data, track users across apps or websites, serve ads, access contacts, or automatically send messages.

Retention and deletion

Local reports and the legacy recovery envelope remain until you delete them. Delete All makes a best-effort attempt to remove app-owned local reports, legacy recovery, drafts, preferences, consent, the installation token, cached artifacts, and the active session. A partial deletion is shown as a failure with a retry path. Local deletion cannot recall data you shared, remove copies in backups or provider infrastructure, or immediately remove short-lived service safety and accounting records. Deleting data or uninstalling the app does not cancel an App Store subscription.
