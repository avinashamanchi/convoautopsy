ConvoAutopsy Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Local reports and sharing

Saved reports are local to your device unless you explicitly share them with your device’s share sheet. ConvoAutopsy does not sell data, track users, serve ads, access contacts, or automatically send messages.

Analysis choices

On-device analysis needs no network transmission. If you choose AI-assisted analysis after giving consent, anonymized speaker labels plus message text are sent to Groq through the ConvoAutopsy proxy. The optional output can be wrong and is not a factual conclusion about people or relationships.

Rate limiting and security

For security and rate limiting, the server HMAC-derives a pseudonymous identifier from the installation token, network signal, and route. A per-digest Durable Object stores only an integer count and window state for a fixed 60-second window. It does not store raw token, IP address, or message text; its alarm removes the counter/window after expiry. No account is required.

Retention and deletion

Local reports remain until you delete them. You can delete individual reports in History, revoke AI consent in Settings, or use Delete all app data to attempt removal of reports, preferences, consent, the installation token, ConvoAutopsy-owned cached exports, and the active in-memory session. If any cleanup fails, the app shows a failure and keeps a retry path rather than claiming deletion succeeded. Local deletion cannot delete data held by Cloudflare or Groq infrastructure; data you explicitly shared outside the app cannot be recalled.

Support

For privacy questions or support, use https://github.com/avinashamanchi/convoautopsy/issues.
