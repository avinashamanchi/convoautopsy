ConvoAutopsy Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Local data and backups

The web app uses a browser-local guest profile. There is no ConvoAutopsy account or backend account for the web app. Saved reports, source text you choose to retain, response drafts, preferences, and consent choices are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly share them. Depending on your browser, iOS, and iCloud settings, local app data may be included in browser, device, or iCloud backups.

On-device and optional AI features

On-device analysis and drafts need no network transmission. For optional AI-assisted analysis or one AI-assisted draft, the exact reviewed message text is sent through Cloudflare to Groq only after you confirm it and consent. The optional output can be incomplete, biased, or wrong and is not a factual conclusion about people or relationships.

Purchases

For purchases and subscription status, RevenueCat processes a pseudonymous app-user ID and purchase and entitlement history. The ConvoAutopsy service may cache verified entitlement status for up to five minutes. RevenueCat and Apple apply their own privacy policies to information they process.

Safety, quotas, and operations

The service HMAC-derives rate and quota identifiers from pseudonymous customer, installation, network, and route signals instead of storing those raw identifiers in its rate and quota keys. It retains bounded quota usage rows, a daily provider budget, two-minute recovery leases, short-lived provider-failure and circuit state, and content-free operational metrics. These records enforce fair-use, reliability, abuse-prevention, and cost limits and do not contain conversation text.

What ConvoAutopsy does not do

ConvoAutopsy does not sell personal data, track users across apps or websites, serve ads, access contacts, or automatically send messages.

Retention and deletion

Local reports remain until you delete them. Delete All makes a best-effort attempt to remove app-owned local reports, drafts, preferences, consent, the installation token, cached artifacts, and the active session. A partial deletion is shown as a failure with a retry path. Local deletion cannot recall data you shared, remove copies in backups or provider infrastructure, or immediately remove short-lived service safety and accounting records. Deleting data or uninstalling the app does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Support

See ConvoAutopsy Support for content-free help or privacy questions. Do not include conversation text or other sensitive information. Use of the app is also governed by the Terms of Use.
