ConvoAutopsy Support

Before reporting a problem

Record the app version, iOS version, action you took, and the error category shown in the app. Do not post conversation text, names, contact details, tokens, screenshots containing private messages, or other sensitive information.

Product boundaries

On-device analysis and drafts work without a purchase or AI. Optional AI features require review, consent, network access, and available fair-use allowance. Generated findings and response drafts can be wrong and must be reviewed.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases from the Convo Pro screen when an eligible purchase is not shown. Purchases cannot be completed in Expo Go; native purchase and restore testing requires a development, TestFlight, or App Store build. Manage or cancel it in Apple subscription settings. For an App Store charge or refund request, use Apple purchase support; Apple decides refund eligibility under its policies. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

For content-free bug reports, use the public project issue tracker.
